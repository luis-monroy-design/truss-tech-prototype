Our story

Who we are

Truss Tech designs and manufactures pre-engineered wood roof and floor trusses in Buford, Georgia, for commercial, multifamily, build-to-rent and senior living projects.

Every package starts from the plans our customers send. Our design team reviews them, asks questions before pricing and produces drawings for submittal; the plant builds and delivers the package to the site.
