How it works

From your plans to your site

Every roof truss package starts from your architectural plans. Our design team lays out the roof, sizes each truss for its span and loads, and sends drawings you can submit.

Production happens in our plant in Buford, Georgia, and the package is delivered to your site ready to set.

7 profiles

Common profiles

Typical shapes we are asked to design. Your plans decide the final profile.

  • Common

    The standard triangle, for most gable roofs.

  • Hip

    Stepped trusses that form a hip roof end.

  • Gable end

    Sits on the end wall and carries the sheathing.

  • Scissor

    Sloped bottom chord for vaulted ceilings.

  • Mono

    Single slope, for sheds, porches and additions.

  • Attic

    Leaves a room or storage space inside the roof.

  • Girder

    Doubled or tripled trusses that carry other trusses.
