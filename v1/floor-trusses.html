How it works

From your plans to your site

Open-web floor trusses span between bearing walls with a wide nailing surface on top and open webs in between, so ducts, pipes and wiring pass through the floor instead of under it.

We design each floor system from your plans, coordinate openings and bearing conditions, and build it in our plant in Buford, Georgia.

3 configurations

Common configurations

Typical shapes we are asked to design. Your plans decide the final profile.

  • Bottom-chord bearing

    Sits on the wall below on its bottom chord.

  • Top-chord bearing

    Hangs from its top chord to keep the floor depth low at the support.

  • With a chase opening

    A clear opening in the web for a main duct run.
