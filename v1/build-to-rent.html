Why BTR developers work with one plant

Build-to-rent communities repeat a few plans across dozens or hundreds of homes. That rewards a supplier who engineers each plan once and produces it consistently, phase after phase.

Truss Tech designs and manufactures in one plant in Buford, Georgia, so your plan set, engineering and production stay in one place from the first quote to the last delivery.
