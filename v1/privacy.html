Privacy policy

How Truss Tech uses the information you send through this website.

What we collect

When you submit plans: your name, company, work email, phone (optional), project type, trusses needed, bid date, job site, notes and the plan files you upload. When you use the contact form: your name, email, company and phone (both optional), the topic and your message. When you email or call us: what you choose to share.

Why we use it

To review your plans, prepare and send your quote, contact you about the project, and keep a record of quotes and orders. We do not sell your information.

Where it is stored

Submissions are stored in our quote system hosted by Supabase in the United States. Plan files are kept in private storage that only Truss Tech staff can open after signing in. Emails are sent through our email provider.

Who can see it

Truss Tech staff who handle quotes, and the service providers that host and send our data on our behalf, only to provide those services.

Spam protection

The form may use a verification service to block automated spam. It processes technical data such as your IP address for that purpose only.

How long we keep it

We keep quotes and plan files only as long as we need them for quoting, orders and our business records.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you sent by writing to info@trusstech.com.
