Fit · One plant

Why multifamily teams work with one plant

Multifamily buildings stack the same floor system level after level, with plumbing, mechanical and electrical runs that have to pass through it. Open-web floor trusses leave room for those runs inside the floor depth.

Truss Tech designs and manufactures in one plant in Buford, Georgia, so the floor and roof packages for each building are engineered together and coordinated with your drawings.

Send the full set for the building or phase. We review it, raise questions on bearing, openings and runs early, and quote the floor and roof packages together.
